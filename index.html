AutoTube

A private desktop tool that publishes the owner's own videos to the owner's own channels.

Not a public service. AutoTube has exactly one user: its developer and owner. There are no customer accounts, no sign-up, and no third-party users. It runs only on the owner's own Windows PC and only ever connects to channels that the owner personally owns and manages.

What it does

AutoTube is a personal automation script (Python) that helps one independent creator publish videos on a regular schedule. On the owner's PC it:

  1. Assembles a video from the owner's own materials: original music generated locally by the owner, licensed royalty-free stock footage (credited in each description), and on-screen text or narration.
  2. Prepares an accurate title, description and tags for that video with a language model that runs locally on the owner's PC. Descriptions credit the stock footage source and the AI-generated music.
  3. Uploads the finished video to the owner's own channel and sets its custom thumbnail, using YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3).
  4. Marks uploads that contain AI-generated music with YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" disclosure.

Channels it manages

AutoTube publishes only to channels owned by the developer:

  • REP BEAT - gym music and workout motivation (Shorts and long workout mixes).
  • A facts / education channel operated by the same owner (planned).

How it uses YouTube API Services

The owner authorizes AutoTube once per channel with Google OAuth 2.0. AutoTube then calls only these YouTube Data API methods, always on the owner's own channel:

MethodPurpose
videos.insertUpload the owner's finished video.
thumbnails.setSet the custom thumbnail of that video.
channels.list (mine=true)Show which channel was just authorized, so the owner can confirm it is the right one.

AutoTube does not read, collect or analyse other people's videos, comments, subscribers or analytics, and it does not automate views, likes, comments or subscriptions.
